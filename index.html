Property Overview & Deal Thesis

3662 N Fredonia Drive represents a rare opportunity to acquire a stabilized, fully occupied four-unit multifamily property in one of Los Angeles' most supply-constrained residential corridors. Located in the hills of Studio City directly adjacent to Universal Studios Hollywood, the property combines institutional-quality location attributes with the operational simplicity of a small-balance four-unit asset — including a newly constructed, fully permitted 2025 ADU with a Certificate of Occupancy issued July 27, 2025.

The current ownership group acquired the property in January 2026 and has since raised rents 3% across all units and completed a series of capital improvements including a new roof, water heater, and exterior railings. The ADU tenant is month-to-month, enabling a buyer to take the unit back at close of escrow — a built-in value-add angle for an owner-user or repositioning buyer. The three legacy units are governed by the Los Angeles Rent Stabilization Ordinance while the 2025 ADU carries full RSO exemption.

At the listed price of $1,575,000, the property is offered at a 5.27% in-place CAP rate and 13.37x GRM — below the Studio City 4-unit comp average of 4.72% CAP and 14.67x GRM, representing a compelling entry point for buyers seeking a turnkey asset in one of LA's highest-barrier submarkets.

Location Overview

Studio City is one of the San Fernando Valley's most desirable residential submarkets, defined by its hillside geography, entertainment industry employer concentration, and severe supply constraints. The Cahuenga Pass corridor — anchored by Universal Studios Hollywood directly across the street from 3662 Fredonia Drive — has historically commanded some of the highest residential rents in the Valley, attracting entertainment professionals and creative industry workers who prioritize proximity to Hollywood while avoiding the density of the urban core.

3662 N Fredonia Drive sits within the Mulholland Scenic Parkway Outer Corridor, a specific plan area that restricts density and preserves the hillside residential character. The property benefits from TOC Tier 3 designation, reflecting its proximity to the Metro B Line Universal/Studio City Station approximately one-quarter mile to the east. This transit overlay creates policy advantages for future development while providing tenants with direct Metro access to the broader LA network. The RSO-exempt 2025 ADU further distinguishes the asset from older hillside inventory.

Employment demand in the submarket is anchored by the entertainment industry ecosystem — Universal Studios, NBCUniversal, Warner Bros. Studios, and the broader Hollywood production corridor collectively employ tens of thousands of workers within a short commute. Ventura Boulevard's retail and dining corridor, Cahuenga Pass access, and direct 101 Freeway connections make the neighborhood highly functional. Median household income within five miles exceeds $93,900 — nearly 25% above the national median — supporting rental demand quality and durable rent growth.
